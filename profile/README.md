
# Hi there 👋, Welcome to Brainz LLC

🌐 **Visit our website → [www.brainz.llc](https://www.brainz.llc)**

Brainz LLC is a **holding company** that runs on two engines: an **Advisory
practice** that fixes broken operations with a repeatable method, and an
**operating portfolio** of real products — each built and run by the operator
who created it, funded by client work rather than outside capital.

## Our Story
In the heart of Bogotá, a decade ago, a vision was born. From a small room to a
global presence, we've built Brainz LLC with a passion for Ruby on Rails and a
dream to be the premier software studio. Our journey took us from local
companies to international clients, and in January 2023 we expanded into the USA.
Today Brainz is a holding company: the Advisory practice and a growing portfolio
of operator-led products, one shared operating loop behind both.

## Our Philosophy
We don't extract IP from the operators who build it — they run it, we back them.
We blend fun, innovation, and world-class quality in everything we do. Our goal
is not just to build software but to create experiences that resonate.
